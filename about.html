About Champaign-Urbana 2600

Like other 2600 meetings, CU2600 is a monthly gathering of people who are interested in technology, how people use it, and how it works. Many regular attendees are especially interested in security, privacy, cryptography, and similar topics. People of any age and skill level are welcome.

Meetings take place on the first Friday of each month on the mezzanine of Harvest Market in Champaign, IL. Someone will be there from 18:00 Central Time until at least 19:00, maybe 20:00. Sometimes an attendee brings a presentation they're going to give at a conference, or they might bring a project for show & tell. Sometimes we talk about problems and try point one another to similar projects that might be helpful. Asking questions and playing with whatever fun technology we brought is encouraged. "We" means you and whoever else shows up, so bring your projects! We do NOT do anything illegal, harm our venue, or judge or harrass fellow attendees.

People attend 2600 meetings for many reasons: learning how things work, meeting other people who share their interests, sharing their knowledge, getting help with a security problem or project, and finding out what other people are working on.
